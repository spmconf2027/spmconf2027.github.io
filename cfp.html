Call for Papers

SPM 2027 · June 21–25, 2027 · Singapore

Scope

SPM 2027 invites submissions of original research on all aspects of solid, shape, and physical modeling, including theory, algorithms, systems, and applications. Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Mathematical and computational foundations of solid and physical modeling
  • Geometric and topological representations
  • Curves, surfaces, splines, and subdivision
  • Implicit, volumetric, and neural representations
  • Mesh generation, meshing, and remeshing
  • Isogeometric analysis and CAD/CAE integration
  • Physics-based modeling and simulation
  • Design and topology optimization
  • Computational design and fabrication
  • Additive manufacturing and 3D printing
  • Multi-material, heterogeneous, and lattice structures
  • Geometric deep learning and data-driven design
  • Reverse engineering and shape reconstruction
  • Feature-based, parametric, and procedural modeling
  • Robust geometric computation and tolerancing
  • Computational geometry and topology
  • Robotics, motion planning, and assembly
  • Applications in biomedicine, geoscience, architecture, and digital media

Submission

Submissions must present previously unpublished, original results that are not simultaneously submitted elsewhere. Papers should be formatted using the Computer-Aided Design journal template. Detailed submission instructions, the page limit, and the submission link will be announced soon.

Submission system: TBA

Publication

Accepted full papers will be published in a special issue of Computer-Aided Design (Elsevier). Papers go through a two-round review process to meet the standards of the journal. At least one author of each accepted paper must register for and present the paper at the conference.

Presentation-Only CAD Papers

Authors of papers published in Computer-Aided Design may also present their work at SPM 2027 as presentation-only papers. The deadline for the notice of intent is April 4, 2027. Eligibility criteria and submission instructions will be announced soon.
