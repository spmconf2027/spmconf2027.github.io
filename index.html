About SPM 2027

The Symposium on Solid and Physical Modeling (SPM) is an annual international conference held with the support of the Solid Modeling Association (SMA). SPM covers all aspects of geometric and physical modeling and their applications in design, analysis, simulation, and manufacturing, as well as in biomedical engineering, geophysics, digital media, and other fields.

SPM 2027 will take place on June 21–25, 2027, at the Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre, as part of the 4th International Geometry Summit (IGS 2027). It is co-located with SGP, SMI, GMP, and SIAM GD, giving attendees a full week of exchange across the applied geometry community.

Accepted full papers will be published in a special issue of Computer-Aided Design (Elsevier). The Pierre Bézier Award of the SMA is traditionally presented at SPM.
