Attending

Registration, venue, and travel

Registration

Registration for SPM 2027 is handled jointly for all conferences of the International Geometry Summit (IGS 2027). Registration details and fees will be posted on the IGS website.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the paper in person.

Venue

Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre
1 Raffles Boulevard, Suntec City, Singapore 039593

The venue is in the Marina Centre district, a short walk from Promenade and Esplanade MRT stations, and close to Marina Bay.

Travel and Visa

Travel, accommodation, and visa information for all summit participants is provided on the IGS 2027 website. Check Singapore's entry requirements with the Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) well in advance of your trip.
